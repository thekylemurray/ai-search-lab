## Hypothesis

Websites with clear structure,
strong entity signals,
descriptive headings,
and crawlable content
perform better in AI retrieval.

## Method

- Evaluate three websites
- Review page structure
- Review metadata
- Review entity clarity
- Review internal linking
- Review citation behavior

## Results

### Evaluation Criteria

Each website will be scored on:

- Content structure
- Heading hierarchy
- Internal linking
- Entity clarity
- Metadata quality
- AI citation likelihood

## Question

Are websites optimized for search engines automatically optimized for AI systems?

## Hypothesis

Traditional SEO improves discoverability, but AI systems reward additional characteristics such as:

- Strong entity clarity
- Better chunking
- Structured content
- Semantic relationships
- Citation-friendly formatting

## Method

Compare:

1. Traditional SEO signals
2. AI retrieval behavior
3. Citation outcomes

## Expected Outcome

AI visibility overlaps with SEO but is not identical.
